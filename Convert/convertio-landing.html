How it runs

Four things happen in order. The first one is the one most tools skip, and skipping it is why their results never tie back to money.

  1. Measurement first

    Before a single word changes, orders and leads are wired back to the version that produced them, server side, so iOS and ad blockers do not eat the signal. Your existing page is registered as the control and stays in rotation permanently.

  2. Versions per angle

    Each ad angle gets a page that finishes the sentence the ad started. An ad about saving time lands on a page about saving time, not on your homepage.

  3. Rendered before it arrives

    The version is chosen and rendered at the edge, so the visitor receives finished HTML. Nothing swaps after load, nothing flickers, and your page speed score goes up rather than down.

  4. Revenue decides

    Allocation follows revenue per visitor, not clicks. A version that gets clicks and no orders loses traffic, which is exactly the outcome click optimization gets backwards.
